The squad sheet

03

Three pandas DataFrames built from StatsBomb's free event data for the 2022 FIFA World Cup: matches, players and shots. They're ready in every editor, along with pd and np. Each was loaded with pd.read_csv("shots.csv"), and you can download the same files to work offline.

Read before kick-off
  • End a drill with the table or number you want on its own line, like the last line of a notebook cell, or store it in a variable called answer.
  • period == 5 is a penalty shootout. Shootout kicks are in shots but don't count as goals in the score, so most drills leave them out with shots[shots["period"] < 5].
  • Combine conditions with & (and) and | (or), and wrap each one in brackets: (a == 1) & (b > 2). Python's and and or don't work on columns.
  • xg is StatsBomb's expected goals for the shot: the chance an average shot from that spot and situation goes in.
  • Text matches are exact and case-sensitive: "Argentina" works, "argentina" doesn't.
