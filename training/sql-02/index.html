The squad sheet

03

The same three tables as session 01. The arrows show how they link: every shot has a match_id that points at one row in matches, and a player_id and team that point at one row in players.

Read before kick-off
  • Give each table a short alias (FROM shots AS s JOIN matches AS m) and prefix columns with it (s.team, m.stage). team on its own is ambiguous once two tables are joined.
  • A player is one row per team in players, so join on both columns: p.player_id = s.player_id AND p.team = s.team.
  • minute counts from 0, as on a match clock: 22 means the 23rd minute.
  • As in session 01, period = 5 is a penalty shootout, and most drills leave it out with s.period < 5.
